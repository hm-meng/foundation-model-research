Research directions

The focus is on understanding the mechanisms behind model learning and using those insights to design better training methods.

01 / OPTIMIZATION

Efficient model training

Investigating optimization algorithms and training strategies that can improve learning efficiency and performance.

02 / MODEL STRUCTURE

Representations and computation

Studying how information and computation evolve across network layers, and how model structure influences learning.

03 / REASONING

Reasoning and post-training

Understanding how post-training changes model behavior and developing methods that strengthen reasoning capabilities.

About

Foundation Model Research is an independent research project focused on the training dynamics, internal structure, and capabilities of modern deep learning models. The goal is to connect empirical observations with principled methods for improving foundation models.
